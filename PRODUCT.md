# Product

## Register
brand

## Users
Participants entering the Nandilath Nippon Lucky Draw. They might be visiting the showroom or scanning a QR code on a mobile device.

## Product Purpose
To collect user registrations for the Nandilath Nippon Lucky Draw securely and present a festive, premium aesthetic that builds excitement.

## Brand Personality
Festive, premium, traditional yet modern, exciting.

## Anti-references
- Boring corporate forms
- Generic SaaS UI
- Dull, flat colors

## Design Principles
- Show, don't tell: Use festive visual motifs (lamps, pookalam, petals) to celebrate the campaign.
- Impeccable contrast and visual hierarchy.
- Delightful micro-interactions and animations.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Ensure text contrast is high and form fields are clearly labeled. Support responsive layouts.
